🔒 How we handle your key

Your API key is encrypted before it ever leaves your browser using AES-256, written to an isolated tenant-specific vault, and only ever decrypted in-memory for the calls Halo makes on your behalf. We never log, replay, or expose your key to third parties. Halo personnel cannot read your key — even in support sessions.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the security of the key you paste here. That means: rotating it if your PMS account is compromised, scoping it to least privilege where your PMS supports scopes, and revoking it from your PMS provider if you stop using Halo. Pasting a key here authorises Halo to call your PMS on your clinic's behalf — bookings, cancellations, notes — and those actions are your actions.

What Halo does (and doesn't)

Halo will only use your key to read availability, write bookings, and update notes scoped to the practitioners and locations you authorise during setup. Halo will not export patient records, will not train any model on your data, and will not use your key for anything outside the receptionist workflows you've enabled.

Termination

You can disconnect at any time from your dashboard. On disconnect we purge your encrypted key from our vault within 60 seconds and emit a confirmation event you can verify in your PMS audit log.
